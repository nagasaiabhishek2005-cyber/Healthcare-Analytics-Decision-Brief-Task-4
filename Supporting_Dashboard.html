Operational interpretation

  • Monitor readmission share using a consistent period and denominator.
  • Review pharmacy revenue trends; profit and stock risk cannot be measured from this file.
  • Route serious safety reports to qualified reviewers under established procedures.
  • Use shared drug names only as an aggregate cross-source comparison, not as a patient-level join.

Limitations and privacy

  • Only 10 patient records, 10 pharmacy transactions and 8 safety reports.
  • Safety reports have no exposed-population denominator; incidence cannot be calculated.
  • No shared patient/event key; no causal or treatment-effect conclusions.
  • Use synthetic data only. Do not add real patient data or attempt re-identification.
  • Suppress or combine small groups in public-facing reports.
